Difference scores over counts are not Gaussian

Entries into one chamber minus another. Responses before minus after. A measure this common should not still be modelled with a distribution that is wrong where it matters.

The problem

What everyone does, including me

Write — about 120 words. Brisk — it is the opening, not the argument.

Set up the measure concretely. Name the paradigms — place conditioning, dipper entries, lever discrimination. These are counts, the difference of two counts is integer-valued and can be negative, and it is routinely handed to ANOVA or a Gaussian mixed model.

Say why it is wrong specifically. Not "the assumptions are violated" — that is the sentence everyone writes and nobody acts on. Say what breaks: the support, the tails, what happens at small n and near zero.

Include yourself. You published under this model. That admission is the reason a reader will trust the rest of the page.

The family

What the right distribution looks like

Write — about 180 words. Keep the taxonomy to a sentence; spend the words on your parameterisation, which is the part that is yours.

Cover: the Panjer difference class; Skellam as Poisson minus Poisson; discrete Laplace as geometric minus geometric; negative-binomial differences with shared or free dispersion; and how they relate — discrete Laplace at β = 1, Skellam as the β → ∞ limit.

Then your parameterisation, which changed for a reason worth telling. Mean μ and variance φ, with φ = √(μ² + δ²) rather than |μ| + δ — the absolute value put a gradient kink exactly where the latent means live. The squared form is smooth, needs no tuning constant because the smoothing scale is the estimated parameter, and it makes δ twice the geometric mean of the two Poisson rates. It also collapses the Bessel argument to a constant, taking special-function evaluations per gradient from 415 to 18.

Be honest about the boundary: you can implement and validate these; you did not derive them. Cite the source.

Reference: Ong, Shimizu & Ng (2008) on the Panjer difference class — [confirm citation].

The comparison

Three models, one data set

Write — about 180 words. The figure carries the argument; prose states the claim and the two failure modes.

Claim: the Gaussian and the Skellam alone cannot produce data shaped like this; the mixture can. State it flatly — all three are visible in the figure and none of it is arguable.

The caption has to carry one thing the plot cannot: panels two and three fail in visually similar ways but for different reasons. The Normal has the wrong support and is symmetric — a structural failure, and discretising actually hides the worst of it. The Skellam has the right support and the right asymmetry but cannot carry the dispersion — it fails on shape, not on kind.

Method note, one sentence: the Gaussian’s integer probabilities come from a continuity correction, so all three panels are drawn on identical geometry and the comparison is exact.

And say what the replicates are: full generative draws — the latent mean is redrawn from its distribution rather than held at its fitted value. Conditioning on fitted latents would make the check nearly unfailable.

Limits

Where it is still wrong

Write — about 150 words. Second-highest-value paragraph on the site.

The numbers are settled: 69 observations sit at a difference of zero, 16.6% of 415. 58 of those — 84% — are sessions with zero entries in both windows. Of those 58, 38 are at the saline dose.

So the excess at zero is structural, not distributional. It is sessions with no occasion setter present and consequently no behaviour to measure. A Skellam has no mechanism for “the animal did not engage” — it only knows about rates. A zero-inflated Skellam is the named extension, and countdiff already specifies zero-inflated, hurdle and zero-deflated variants of every family.

Also state here: adapt_delta, the seed and draw count, that no formal model comparison was run and why a failed PPC is the right instrument, and that the latent mean is written centred for legibility.

Tooling

countdiff

Write — about 100 words

What it is: regression families for integer differences of counts as custom brms response families — Skellam, discrete Laplace, negative-binomial difference, each with zero-inflated, hurdle and zero-deflated variants.

State its actual condition. In development, nothing validated. That sentence costs nothing and protects completely, and a reader who finds an unvalidated package behind a confident claim will discount everything else on the site.
