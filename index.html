Write — about 180 words, three short paragraphs. The collaboration line is one sentence, not a paragraph.

The one thing to state outright: your title says neuroscience, your days say statistics. A reader who does not learn that in the first two sentences will misfile you.

Then: how you got here — self-taught from primary literature and Stan documentation, started by running into difference scores over counts being modelled as Gaussian.

Then: that you draw your own figures and write your lab's analysis tooling, without explaining the connection. Let the reader notice. Close with one sentence on the collaboration line — everything here is yours, and where a project was built with help the page says so.

Do not characterise yourself. No "passionate", no "rigorous". Name what you do and let the pages carry the adjectives. Hardest page on the site because it is the only one about you rather than about work — resist explaining what the reader is about to see.
