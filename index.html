Davin Peart

PhD student in behavioural neuroscience at the University of Guelph. Most of what I actually do is statistical modelling.

Selected work

  • Documenting a model so someone can disagree with it Bayesian workflow

    Seven models, each specified, prior-checked, diagnosed, predicted and tested on a single page. The generative diagram is drawn by hand for every one — it is the part you cannot produce from a brm() call.

  • Difference scores over counts are not Gaussian Methods · in progress

    Entries into one chamber minus another; responses before minus after. Routinely fitted as continuous and symmetric, which is wrong exactly at the tails. What the Skellam and its relatives do instead, and what it changes on data I have already published.

  • Figures Illustration

    Signalling cascades, circuit anatomy, task schematics, graphical abstracts. Drawn with splines, one object at a time.
