Methods

Statistical. Bayesian multivariate non-linear mixed modelling in Stan and brms; prior specification on latent parameters; posterior predictive checking; Savage-Dickey Bayes factors; HDI and region-of-practical-equivalence decision procedures; NUTS configuration and diagnosis; simulation-based calibration. Weighted network and graph analysis with permutation inference. PCA, GLM-PCA, Procrustes rotation, principal component regression. GLMs and GLMMs; repeated-measures designs with Greenhouse–Geisser correction; estimated marginal means with FDR-corrected contrasts.

Software. R (tidyverse, ggplot2, brms, glmmTMB, lme4, emmeans, igraph, vegan), Stan (rstan, cmdstanr), SPSS, GraphPad Prism. R package development with roxygen2, testthat and continuous integration. git and GitHub. ImageJ / Fiji.

Laboratory. Rat jugular catheterization, ovariectomy and anaesthesia. Intravenous cocaine self-administration, cocaine-primed reinstatement, extinction, conditioned place preference, Pavlovian occasion setting, interoceptive drug discrimination, locomotor activity, estrous cycle staging. Immunohistochemistry for c-Fos, perfusion, sectioning, imaging and automated cell quantification.
